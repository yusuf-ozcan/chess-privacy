Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Chess Premium handles your information. Your privacy is a priority, and the App is designed to be a secure, minimalist, and private environment.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Chess Premium is built with a privacy-first approach. The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal identification information (PII) to external servers. All gameplay data is processed and stored locally on your device:

  • Move history and game analysis
  • Stockfish engine calculations
  • User settings and theme preferences
  • Achievements and trophy progress

No account creation is required to use the App.

2. Local Processing (Stockfish)

The Stockfish integration operates entirely on your device's local hardware. No game data or board positions are sent to external cloud services for analysis.

3. Third-Party & Data Retention

The App does not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Since all data is stored locally, your history remains on your device until you delete the App.
