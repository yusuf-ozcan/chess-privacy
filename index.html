Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Chess Premium (the "App") handles your information. Your privacy is a priority, and the App is designed to be a secure, minimalist, and private environment for playing chess.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Chess Premium is built with a privacy-first approach. The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal identification information (PII) to external servers. All gameplay, including:

  • Move history and game analysis,
  • Stockfish engine calculations,
  • User settings and theme preferences,
  • Achievements and trophy progress,

is processed and stored locally on your device. No account creation is required to use the App.

2. Local Processing (Stockfish Engine)

The Stockfish integration operates entirely on your device's local hardware (CPU/GPU). No game data or board positions are sent to external cloud services for analysis.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising frameworks, or tracking SDKs that collect your data. Your interactions within the App remain private to you.

4. Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally, your game history and settings remain on your device as long as the App is installed. You can clear this data at any time by deleting the App from your device.

5. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
